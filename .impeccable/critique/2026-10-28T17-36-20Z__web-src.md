## Priority Issues

1. [P1] Keyboard focus is invisible on every custom control. `all: 'unset'` on filter values, tag chips, the name/open button, Copyable, CheckFilter trigger, distribution labels and bars, and "+N" removes the focus ring with no `:focus-visible` replacement. Fix: shared focus-visible style. Command: polish.
2. [P1] Resources table overflows its panel at 1366px: "最近变化" is clipped at the right edge with no scroll cue; rows are 3 lines tall (wrapped ARN + stacked chips); EIM, account and account type take three columns. Fix: merge account columns, single-line ARN with ellipsis, chips on one line, sticky last column or tighter widths. Commands: layout, distill.
3. [P1] Coverage matrix colours are hard-coded light hex: in dark theme the "never scanned" cells are bright white tiles, the loudest element on the page is the empty state. Fix: theme tokens, quiet "never" state. Command: polish.
4. [P2] Filters live in a separate card from the table they scope; 9 controls in an auto-fill grid with orphaned placement ("清除全部" floats mid-row). On phones 7 stacked controls push every result below the fold. Fix: one toolbar attached to the results, secondary filters behind a toggle on narrow screens. Command: layout.
5. [P2] Flat hierarchy: page h1 (HeadingMedium) and panel headings (HeadingSmall) are near-equal; subtitle hugs the title; KPI tiles are the hero-number template with low-information values (EIM 2, 账号 2); job page nests Stat cards inside a Panel. Commands: typeset, distill.
